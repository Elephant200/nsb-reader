NSB Reader provides high-school National Science Bowl practice.

Practice tossups, bonuses, or paired questions on your own, or share a private room for individual multiplayer practice.

The interface uses QBReader, created by Geoffrey Wu and its contributors. Answer checking uses qb-answer-checker.

Questions come from the U.S. Department of Energy's high-school sample questions. Use the flag beside a question to report an incorrect answer or formatting problem.

Practice instructions and keyboard shortcuts

No account is required. Preferences and starred questions are saved in your browser; multiplayer scores last for the room session.
