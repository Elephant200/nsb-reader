Practice instructions

Choose Tossups, Bonuses, or Both under Play. Use the settings to choose categories, sets, packets, and reading speed. Press Next to begin.

Buzz to stop a tossup and enter your answer. For multiple choice, enter the W/X/Y/Z letter or the full correct choice. If you include both, they must agree. Each choice appears on its own line.

Correct tossups score 4 points. Wrong interruptions score -4, and wrong answers after the question ends score 0. Correct bonuses score 10. Use the grading correction if an answer is marked incorrectly.

Both includes the bonus after a missed tossup unless you disable that option. Multiplayer bonuses are optional and belong to the player who answered the tossup correctly.

Create a private multiplayer room and share its URL. The room owner controls settings and advancement, and can transfer ownership from a player's menu.

Keyboard shortcuts: Space to buzz, N for next, P to pause, and E for settings. Shortcuts are inactive while typing.

Use the star to save a question in this browser, or the flag to report it with a reason.
